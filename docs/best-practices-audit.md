# Best-practices audit — checklist vs. implementation

**Date:** 2026-09-19 · **Subject:** the character lane (`addon/forge/tools/rigforge_*`, `rigcheck`,
`correctives`; `mcp/forge_mcp/pipeline.py`; `assistant/system_prompt.md`) · **Method:** read-only.
Nothing in this pass ran Blender, opened a port or changed a file other than this one.

## Why this document exists

The house law converts a defect into a gate **after** a human sees it. The wip-14 audit
(`projects/werewolf/renders/review-wip14/00-VERDICT.json`) produced five defects in one wave, and
every one of them was a **missing practice** rather than a botched implementation:

| wip-14 defect | measured | the practice that was never written down |
|---|---|---|
| leg IK stretch on a planted walk | left leg chain **+261.7 mm = +32.58%** at f33 | game rigs ship with IK stretch **off** |
| rest stance at full reach | **0.9984** of chain length; 1.3 mm headroom on an 800 mm leg | rig the stance with the knees **flexed** |
| walk travel on the torso control | body travels 641.6 mm on `torso`; seam open **281 mm** | root motion goes on the **root** |
| JCM curves keyed past π | peaks at **3.953 / 3.9886 rad**; deliver ≤ **71.5%** | `ROTATION_DIFF` has domain `[0, π]` |
| jump anticipation that does not read | hip setback **0.00 mm**, load **0.333 s**, drop **6.9%** | a countermovement goes **down and back** |

The implementation of what *was* specified is, on the evidence below, unusually strong — the
tooling is deterministic, headless, self-documenting and measured, and most constants in it carry
the experiment that chose them. The failure mode of this codebase is not sloppy code. It is
**a checklist that was never assembled**. This document assembles it.

## How to read the status column

| status | means |
|---|---|
| **GATED** | implemented, and a named machine gate carries a pass/attention/fail verdict on it |
| **UNGATED** | implemented and/or measured and reported, but no verdict blocks on the number |
| **PARTIAL** | implemented for some limbs, clips or lanes and not others |
| **ABSENT** | no implementation and no measurement |
| **COVERED-IN-FLIGHT** | one of the six gates (plus their authoring counterparts) landing concurrently — already present in `rigcheck.py` / `rigforge_anim.py` / `correctives.py` with tests in `addon/tests/headless_animgates.py` |

Severity is *the defect the gap produces*, not the difficulty of the fix:
**Critical** = ships a broken asset silently · **High** = user-visible defect that a gate would
have caught · **Medium** = defect that shows up under a specific clip or engine · **Low** = craft
polish or documentation drift.

All file references are absolute-by-repo-root, e.g. `addon/forge/tools/rigcheck.py:2579`.

## B. Rest pose / stance conventions

| # | Practice | Source | Status | Where it lives / what is missing | Severity | Remedy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | Anatomical **pre-bend**: a knee apexes forward, an elbow backward, before anything is animated | Standard rigging practice; required because three collinear points give IK no plane and Rigify's pole angle comes from that plane | **GATED** | `rigforge_landmarks.prebend_joint` (`rigforge_landmarks.py:2166`), `PREBEND_FRACTION = 0.03`, `PREBEND_DIRECTION = {"knee": +1.0, "elbow": -1.0}` (`:430`–`442`), clamped to `PREBEND_SECTION_FRACTION = 0.667` of the limb's own cross-section so the joint cannot be pushed out of the flesh. Gated by `rig_check.bend_direction`. Documented with the failure it came from (a werewolf knee 25.9 mm **behind** the hip–ankle line). | — | none — this is the model for every other item in this table |
| B2 | Rest stance stands with the **knees flexed** (≈10–20°), so IK has reach headroom | Game-rig convention: a straight-leg rest pose has no headroom, so the first hip translation over a planted foot either pops or stretches; Rigify's own stretch behaviour is the amplifier ([Blender Manual — Rigify](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/addons/rigging/rigify/index.html)) | **COVERED-IN-FLIGHT for detection · ABSENT for authoring** | Detection landed: `rigcheck.ik_reach_headroom_rest` (`rigcheck.py:2756`), thresholds `warn 0.98 / fail 1.00` (`IK_REACH_THRESHOLDS`, `:2463`), wired as a `rig_check` placement gate (`:1851`), with a `says` string that literally reads *"Rest the knees at 15-20 degrees."* **Nothing authors that stance.** `PREBEND_FRACTION = 0.03` is a *lateral* offset of 3% of the limb's span — on an 800 mm leg that shortens the hip-to-ankle span by well under a millimetre. Add `rest_stance: flexed \| straight` to `_character_template`. Then `ik_reach_headroom_rest` becomes a gate that **passes** instead of a permanent red light, and the three per-clip crouch workarounds become optional. |
| B3 | Bind pose chosen and recorded — **A-pose** for a clothed/shouldered figure, T-pose for retarget compatibility | A T-pose binds the deltoid at full abduction, which is the worst place to sample its rest shape; A-pose is the game convention, T-pose the mocap/humanoid-retarget convention | **ABSENT** | `rigforge_landmarks` carries a *T-pose assumption* in its axis solving (`rigforge_landmarks.py:1136`, `:1506`, `:2318`) but nothing measures or records the actual shoulder abduction, and the task-config has no field for it. A character delivered in A-pose will silently take the T-pose axis assumptions. | **Medium** | Add `bind_pose: a_pose \| t_pose` to `_character_template`; `rig_check` reports measured shoulder abduction in degrees and **fails** when it disagrees with the setting by more than 15°. |
| B4 | Feet flat on the sole plane at rest (mesh lowest Z ≈ 0) | Engine convention: the character origin is the floor contact | **PARTIAL** | `rigcheck.foot_height` (`:1285`) gates the *ankle* height and the toe chain against the sole. `_evaluated_z_extent` (`:3131`) exists and is used for the jump's floor check and `anticipation_reads`' `floor_mm >= -1.0 mm`. **No gate applies it to the rest pose.** The wip-14 rest mesh sat at +0.7 mm — fine, unmeasured. | **Medium** | New `rig_check` placement gate **`sole_plane`**: rest evaluated mesh min Z within **±2 mm** of 0, using the `_evaluated_z_extent` already written. Three lines against a whole class of "the character floats / sinks in Godot" reports. |
| B5 | Stance width and foot parallelism recorded | Locomotion craft: stride and sway defaults are only meaningful against a known stance width | **ABSENT** | `contact_points` / `_diameter` know where the feet are but nothing measures the gap between them or their yaw at rest. | **Low** | Report `stance_width_mm` and per-foot yaw in `rig_check`'s `foot_height` block. | B6 | Hands rest with fingers slightly curled, thumb opposed | Rigging convention: a splayed rest hand makes every grip pose fight the bind | **ABSENT** | `rigcheck.hand_containment` (`:1092`) gates whether the hand **bone** points the way the palm does and stays inside it — the palm, not the fingers. | B7 | The mesh is bound at the rest pose (no posed bind) | Linear blend skinning is defined against the bind pose; a posed bind bakes the pose into the inverse-bind matrices | **GATED** (by construction) | The chain is landmarks → metarig → generate → skin, all at rest; `rigcheck`'s rest regions are taken from **evaluated** rest coordinates rather than `mesh.vertices`, precisely so a shape-keyed mesh is compared against the shape the armature deforms (`rigcheck.py` module docstring, "What 'the mesh' means here"). | — | none |

## D. Walk / run cycle craft

| # | Practice | Source | Status | Where it lives / what is missing | Severity | Remedy |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | The planted foot is **world-locked**: key the foot IK target, not leg FK rotations | Body-mechanics convention; measured here at 189 mm of slide for FK legs against 1.1 mm for IK (`task_config.py:135`) | **GATED** | `cmd_rigforge_walk` keys foot IK targets (`rigforge_anim.py:1841`ff); `rigcheck.cmd_animation_check` measures the **ball of the foot** (`CONTACT_CANDIDATES` prefers `DEF-toe.*` head) with `FOOT_SLIDE_THRESHOLDS = {"drift_mm": {"ok": 5.0, "attention": 20.0}}` (`rigcheck.py:2044`). | D2 | Pose-to-pose blocking on the four classic keys: **contact, down, passing, up** | Richard Williams, *The Animator's Survival Kit* — the four positions of a walk ([summary](https://bridgetharveyani210.wordpress.com/2014/12/17/walk-cycle-richard-williams-animators-survival-kit/)) | **PARTIAL (doc drift)** | The section header claims the command *"authors the four classic keys of a walk — contact, down, pass, up"* (`rigforge_anim.py:1541`). nobody fails on, a limb nobody covers, or a practice nobody wrote down.**

The single most important structural finding is not in any one row. It is that **every gate in this
repository measures the Blender scene, and the product is a `.glb`.** B-Bones, `STRETCH_TO` and
shape-key drivers are all live when `rig_check` measures, and none of them exist in the file Godot
loads. Until G15 exists, every green verdict in this codebase is a statement about a rig, not about
an asset.

## The ten highest-value unimplemented items

1. **Rig the rest stance with flexed knees.** *(B2 · game-rig convention / Rigify stretch behaviour)* Prevents: every leg-stretch, foot-pop and hip-reach defect downstream — the rig audited at 0.9984 has 1.3 mm of headroom on an 800 mm leg. **Remedy:** `stance_knee_deg` (default 12°) in `rigforge_landmarks` targeting rest span ≤ 0.97 of chain length, plus `rest_stance` in the character task-config; turns `ik_reach_headroom_rest` from a permanent red light into a pass.
2. **Verify the exported `.glb`, not the scene.** *(G15 · the definition of a gate)* Prevents: every silent Blender-vs-engine divergence, starting with B-Bone curvature and `STRETCH_TO`. **Remedy:** new `export_verify` command — re-import the `.glb` via `model.import_gltf`, assert joint/animation/morph parity, compare evaluated meshes per frame; **≤ 2 mm attention, ≤ 5 mm fail**; wired into the `export` stage gate.
3. **Flatten B-Bones on the export rig.** *(A3 · [glTF-Blender-IO #708](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Blender-IO/issues/708))* Prevents: 10-segment limb curvature that `rig_check` credits and Godot cannot render — the verdict's 35.3 mm of "beyond plain vertex-group skinning". **Remedy:** `build_deform_rig` sets `bbone_segments = 1` and reports `bbone_flattened`; paired with #2 as its measurement.
4. **Default `IK_Stretch = 0` on the rig, not just on three clips.** *(A2 · [Rigify limb rigs](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/addons/rigging/rigify/index.html))* Prevents: the wip-14 leg stretch recurring on any hand-keyed or retargeted clip, which is every clip the tools did not author. **Remedy:** `rigforge_generate_rig` writes it; new `rig_check.ik_stretch_default` placement gate against the task-config policy.
5. **Name the four new `animation_check` gates in the pipeline's `animate` stage.** *(H1 · this codebase's own law)* Prevents: the entire wip-14 wave passing a build plan that only ever asked for foot slide. **Remedy:** add `animation_check.deformation_gate` and `animation_check.travel_on_root` to `pipeline.py:196`, and the four rig-time gates to the `rig`/`skin`/`correctives` rows. Pure data edit; highest value per line in this document.
6. **Gate the influence budget and normalisation.** *(C1/C2/C3 · [glTF 2.0 spec](https://registry.khronos.org/glTF/specs/2.0/glTF-2.0.html) — four joints per set, weights sum to 1.0)* Prevents: a fifth influence dropped silently by the engine, or a vertex scaled by unnormalised weights. **Remedy:** `rig_check.influence_budget` rolling up the three numbers `weight_report` already computes, in the `skin` stage gate.
7. **`travel_on_root`.** *(D3 · [Godot RootMotionView](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_rootmotionview.html))* Prevents: the exact wip-14 walk — 641.6 mm of travel on the torso control, seam open 281 mm — by naming the cause rather than the symptom. **Remedy:** `animation_check` fails when `body_travel_mm` is significant and `body_bone != "root"`; it already computes both.
8. **Gate the deformation stack.** *(A9/F7 · [Auto-Rig Pro game export](https://lucky3d.fr/auto-rig-pro/doc/ge_export_doc.html); glTF is linear-blend only)* Prevents: a Preserve Volume or Corrective Smooth bind that looks better in Blender and ships worse to Godot, invisibly, past all ten current placement gates. **Remedy:** `rig_check.deformation_stack` — one armature modifier, preserve-volume off, envelopes off, nothing else in the chain; it is the verdict's own probe promoted to a gate.
9. **`corrective_never_overshoots` — the seventh proposed gate that did not land.** *(F4 · the wip-14 verdict's own `proposed_gates`)* Prevents: a hand-sculpted JCM that pushes past rest volume, which is the next defect this lane produces now that the domain bug is fixed. **Remedy:** at each pose `rig_check` already takes, hull volume with keys live must lie in `[keys_off, rest]`; fail on any crossing. Measured passing today — cheapest possible time to add it.
10. **Judge the punch's anticipation and its kinetic chain.** *(E10/E11/E12 · Disney's twelve principles; Williams on timing)* Prevents: on the other combat clip, the identical failure the jump had — an anticipation that is physically present and invisible — plus a punch that starts at the arm or is still accelerating at contact. **Remedy:** generalise `anticipation_window` to take an action peak instead of a takeoff and add `animation_check.kinetic_chain_order` (pelvis < chest < shoulder < fist peaks, and `peak_speed_frame < strike_frame`), measured in `animation_check` so it works on retargeted clips too.
